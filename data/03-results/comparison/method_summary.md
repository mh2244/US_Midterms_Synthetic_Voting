For 31 October, adding polling and historical information lowers the Republican control probability from 90% in the synthetic-only forecast to 37%. The no-LLM benchmark gives 34%, so including synthetic responses moves the combined forecast toward greater Republican support. Synthetic estimates receive an average weight of 19% in polled states and 17% in unpolled states. The remaining weight goes to state polling averages or, where unavailable, the 2024 CES vote share plus uniform swing. The combined result is therefore driven mainly by these outside estimates. Without the bias correction, the synthetic shares receive less weight (an average of 11% in polled states) but keep their Republican lean, and the probability of Republican control is 42%: the choice between the two methods alone moves it by 5 points.
