For 31 October, adding polling and historical information lowers the Republican control probability from 90% in the synthetic-only forecast to 42%. The no-LLM benchmark gives 34%, so including synthetic responses moves the combined forecast toward greater Republican support. Synthetic estimates receive an average weight of 11% in polled states and 9% in unpolled states. The remaining weight goes to state polling averages or, where unavailable, the 2024 CES vote share plus uniform swing. The combined result is therefore driven mainly by these outside estimates.
