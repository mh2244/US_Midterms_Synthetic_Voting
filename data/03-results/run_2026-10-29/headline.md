## The Democratic caucus is favoured to control the Senate, 52 seats to 48

We combine synthetic responses from four language models with polling data. We forecast the 35 Senate races in 2026. To estimate which party will control the Senate, we add the 65 seats not up for election: 31 held by Republicans, 32 by Democrats, and two by independents, Bernie Sanders and Angus King, who caucus with Democrats.

Across the simulations, the Democratic caucus reaches the 51-seat threshold for control without support from newly elected independents in 59 per cent of outcomes. Republicans retain control in 39 per cent. Republicans need 50 seats because the Republican vice president can break a 50–50 tie. The central 90 per cent of simulated Republican seat totals ranges from 40 to 56, showing the uncertainty around the forecast.
