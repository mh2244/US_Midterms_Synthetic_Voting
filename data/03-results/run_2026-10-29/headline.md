## The Democratic caucus is favoured to control the Senate, 52 seats to 48

We combine synthetic responses from four language models with polling data to forecast the 35 Senate races in 2026. To estimate which party will control the Senate, we add the 35 race predictions to the 65 seats not up for election: 31 held by Republicans, 32 by Democrats, and two by independents, Bernie Sanders and Angus King, who caucus with Democrats.

The probability that the Democratic caucus reaches the 51-seat threshold for control, without support from newly elected independents, is 59 per cent; the probability that Republicans retain control is 39 per cent. Republicans need 50 seats because the Republican vice president can break a 50–50 tie. In 90 per cent of our simulations, Republicans win between 40 and 56 seats, showing the uncertainty around the forecast.
